Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

The Bakkal application ("App") is designed to operate locally on your device. This privacy policy explains how your information is handled when you use our application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The Bakkal application stores user information (such as email addresses or login credentials) exclusively on your local device database (local storage) for basic app functionality and user session management.

Email data and personal information are never transmitted to any external server, cloud infrastructure, or third-party service over the internet. All data remains strictly on your device.

2. Data Sharing

Since all data is stored locally on your device, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third parties.

3. Security

Because your data is kept locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and its local storage.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

5. Contact Us
